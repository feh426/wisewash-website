Three components, one verified file

From raw exports to a supportable value.

Membership verification

What the membership base is worth. Membership is the largest single driver of an express wash’s value and the hardest to see in a report. The platform reads the roster, the wash log, the billing history and the point-of-sale audit log, and tests every row.

It identifies

  • Duplicate accounts and plates with no active vehicle
  • Dormant accounts still counted as members
  • Paused accounts and churn not yet surfaced
  • Promotional spikes and price step-up overhang
  • Conversion and back-office adjustments

It produces

  • Verified member count and collectible recurring revenue
  • Lifetime value by plan and commitment length, using survival analysis
  • A bridge from the reported base to the verified base
  • A verification workbook a lender or accountant can open and test

Operating benchmarking

Where the next dollar of earnings comes from. The platform scores a site or portfolio against published regional benchmarks, identifies each area of concern and prices every lever: the earnings it is worth, the ramp and the payback. Every input and assumption can be overridden, and the change flows through the whole plan.

It prices

  • Dunning, churn and membership conversion
  • Pricing, plan structure and retail ticket
  • Labor and each cost line
  • Water reclaim, capacity and throughput
  • Marketing

It produces

  • A benchmark scorecard for the site or portfolio
  • Areas of concern ranked by the earnings at stake
  • A sequenced plan with anticipated results, ramp and payback for each lever

Full underwrite

From raw exports to a supportable value, in one file. Sixteen modules carry a site from raw point-of-sale exports to a supportable value, a financeable capital structure and a written record of every assumption behind them.

It covers

  • Membership verification at row level
  • Trade area capture within three and five miles, with the permit pipeline weighted
  • P&L normalization, with add-backs credited by category
  • Current and future value on sourced multiples
  • Capital structure and debt service across the full term
  • Scenarios, Monte Carlo simulation, a risk register and an override register

It produces

  • An underwriting report and workbook
  • A supportable value and a downside case
  • A lender package organized for a credit process
  • Side-by-side comparison and portfolio roll-up across sites

Special situations

A clear path forward for car wash assets under pressure.

WiseWash steps in when a car wash loan defaults, a site loses members or an operator leaves. We assess the asset, keep it running and carry out a plan to stabilize it, working alongside the lender, owner and their advisors.

Discuss a challenged asset
Situations we handle

Six ways a car wash ends up on a lender’s watch list.

Loans in default
Payment or covenant defaults where the lender needs an operator’s view of the collateral.
Declining performance
Sites losing members, volume or margin faster than the market around them.
Operator departures
An owner or management team that has stepped away, leaving a site without leadership.
Receiverships
Court-appointed or private receiverships that need a car wash operator in place.
Portfolio pressure
Platforms with sites that are weighing on the whole portfolio.
Hard deadlines
A maturity, refinance or sale that needs a verified operating record on a short timeline.
The path forward

Assess, agree, carry it through.

Assess the asset

A condition, value and going-concern review of the site, with the membership base, equipment and financial position verified rather than assumed.

Agree the plan

Priorities, responsibilities, reporting and capital needs set with the lender, owner and their advisors, with verified projections for forbearance or workout discussions.

Carry it through

Interim management that keeps the site open, staffed and serving its members, alone or alongside a receiver, then stabilization and a verified record for a refinance, recapitalization or sale.

Alongside your team

We work within the lender’s or owner’s process, alongside their advisors, and report against the plan every month until the situation is resolved.

Operators, not observers

Our operating leadership has run car wash platforms of more than one hundred locations, and every assessment runs on the WiseWash analytics platform.
